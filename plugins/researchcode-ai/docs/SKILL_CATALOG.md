# Skill catalog

- **research-discovery** — Discover papers with public code or data and verifiable links
- **journal-ranking** — Verify FT50 and AJG 3/4/4-star journal classification by edition
- **replication-audit** — Assess reproducibility of published empirical code and data
- **research-methods** — Design and check econometric models and research methods
- **reviewer-engine** — Independent AI reviewer and falsification protocol for empirical accounting/finance
- **data-licensing** — Verify and preserve data access rights for academic research
- **sox-testing** — Prepare auditable SOX 404 control test designs and workpapers
- **audit-support** — Support accounting audit documentation and evidence quality assessment
- **reconciliation** — Reconcile GL to bank, subledger and intercompany balances
- **journal-entry-prep** — Prepare proposed journal entries with evidence and accounting policy
- **variance-analysis** — Calculate and explain period variances without unsupported causal claims
- **financial-statements** — Analyze P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and IFRS/GAAP accounting ratios
- **close-management** — Organize financial close tasks with dependency and evidence gates
- **validate-data** — Check datasets, definitions and calculations for research integrity
- **statistical-analysis** — Create transparent R/Python/Stata statistical analysis and robustness plans
- **sql-analysis** — Write safe read-only SQL for linked research datasets
- **dashboards** — Build source-backed research and audit analytics dashboards
- **capital-iq-licensed** — Optional S&P Capital IQ/Kensho workflow requiring a separate license
- **research-ethics** — Check privacy, AI disclosure and research integrity before dissemination

This bundle provides original, independently written workflows. See `docs/CREDITS.md` for external inspiration. Other providers’ skills are not copied or installed by this package.
