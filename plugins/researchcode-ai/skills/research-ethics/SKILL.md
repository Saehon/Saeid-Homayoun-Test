# Research Ethics

## Safeguards
Respect copyrights, provider terms, research participant confidentiality and GDPR where applicable. Do not share identifying firm-level nonpublic data, unpublished referee reports, confidential workpapers, API keys or restricted rankings through public GitHub, chat logs or plugin telemetry. Apply disclosure and authorship requirements of the target journal. Require human review of AI-generated references and empirical claims before submission; flag suspected fabrication rather than filling gaps with plausible-looking data.
