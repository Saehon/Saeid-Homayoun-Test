# Sox Testing

## Procedure
Identify entity, fiscal period, control objective, relevant assertion, risk, COSO principle, control owner and evidence. Distinguish walkthrough, design effectiveness and operating effectiveness. Plan an appropriately justified sampling approach, population completeness validation, control frequency, exceptions and extent of testing; do not invent a mandatory sample size.

Document each sample ID, source evidence, execution timestamp, performer, reviewer, exception and compensating control. Classify potential deficiencies using likelihood and magnitude as a preliminary judgment; leave final severity classification to qualified auditors. Do not declare SOX compliance or give audit opinions from generated test plans. Cite applicable PCAOB standards or COSO edition after verification.
