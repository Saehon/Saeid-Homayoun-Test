# Financial Statements

## Procedure
Confirm reporting framework (IFRS, US GAAP, Swedish K2/K3), consolidation scope, period, accounting changes, presentation currency and restatements. Map raw account codes to income statement, balance sheet and cash flow headings using an approved chart of accounts. Check balance-sheet identity, cash-flow tie-out, year-over-year variance and non-recurring items. Explain EBITDA and alternative performance metrics separately from IFRS measures. Do not mix EBIT, EBITA and EBITDA and label reconciliations and unavailable numbers.
