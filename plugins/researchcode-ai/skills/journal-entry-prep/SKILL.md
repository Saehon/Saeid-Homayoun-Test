# Journal Entry Prep

## Procedure
Identify entity, local GAAP/IFRS basis, accounting period, transaction substance, evidence date, recognition and measurement. Generate debit and credit lines whose totals agree, accounting policy citation, calculation, source-document reference and reviewer approval slot. Separate estimates and assumptions from verified balances. For revenue or leases reference the correct applicable standards and clarify the contract facts.

Do not create or post an entry in ERP; drafts remain unposted until authorized human approval. For accruals, depreciation and deferrals include reversal/amortization schedule and relevant cut-off. If provided inputs are incomplete, output an explicit missing-data checklist.
