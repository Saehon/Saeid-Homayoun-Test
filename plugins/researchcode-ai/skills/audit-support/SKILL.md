# Audit Support

## Audit evidence protocol
For each material assertion identify relevant IFRS/US GAAP requirements and relevant ISA/PCAOB audit standards, check effective editions. Map risk to population, tests of controls, substantive procedures, corroboration, completeness and appropriateness of evidence. Use audit trail with provenance and source-document checks. Check management override, estimates, related parties and fraud risks separately.

Always separate generated example workpaper, reviewed workpaper and actual audit evidence. Flag uncertainty about professional judgment; propose alternative procedures where material risks remain. Preserve audit evidence confidentiality and reviewer approval. Do not export working papers or auditor-client data to external services without explicit authority.
